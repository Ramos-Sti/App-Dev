# Lucifer

![Lucifer](https://your-direct-image-link.jpg)

## About
**Genre:** Fantasy, Crime, Comedy-Drama  
**Network:** Fox, then Netflix  
**Years:** 2016 – 2021  
**Lead Actor:** Tom Ellis  

*Bored and unhappy with being the Lord of Hell, Lucifer Morningstar leaves for Los Angeles, opens a nightclub, and starts helping the LAPD solve crimes.*

## Main Characters
- **Lucifer Morningstar** – the charming devil, played by Tom Ellis
- **Chloe Decker** – the LAPD detective he partners with
- **Mazikeen** – his loyal demon friend
- **Amenadiel** – Lucifer's angel brother
  - He tries to send Lucifer back to Hell
- **Linda Martin** – Lucifer's therapist
- **Ella Lopez** – the forensic scientist
